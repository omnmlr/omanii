# User research

Study whether people understand and use OMANII without being misled. Qualitative feedback answers questions about understanding, behavior and trust; technical measurement evidence answers whether a measured benefit is real. Neither substitutes for the other.

## Questions worth testing

- Can people explain spatial visualizations and distinguish observed, interpolated and unknown areas?
- Do they understand uncertainty and “No clear better location found,” or mistake sharp boundaries/colors for precision?
- Can they distinguish signal strength and Wi-Fi link speed from actual connection quality?
- Do recommendations lead to appropriate, useful actions? Verify claimed connection improvements separately with valid measurements.
- What do people think scores measure, how do they compare them, and do they understand incomplete results and endpoint/profile limits?
- Do people understand permission requests, accessed data and what remains possible after denial?
- What creates justified trust or false certainty, including animations, labels and apparent live updates?
- For later Boost and gaming-oriented scoring concepts, what effects do people expect? Could the presentation imply unsupported optimization or game-server accuracy?

These questions do not authorize implementing Boost, gaming formulas, new permissions or analytics.

## Keep studies small and interpretable

Choose a learning objective, relevant participant context, identified prototype/build and short realistic task. Observe actions and ask people to explain their interpretation before explaining the intended meaning. Capture misunderstanding and disagreement, not just preferences.

Record question, recruitment/context and limitations, consent, procedure, prototype version, observed behavior, participant interpretation, researcher interpretation and next question. Label illustrative/demo content; never present it as live network evidence. Avoid unnecessary identity, room imagery or network details.

Document differences in visuals, wording and uncertainty states across prototypes. Keep accuracy checks separate from comprehension evidence. Small qualitative samples can reveal problems but do not establish population prevalence, willingness to pay or technical improvement.

Product studies may later examine care, action, return, sharing or willingness to pay under explicit scope; this guidance adds no tracking or billing behavior. Summarize real evidence as [findings](../findings/README.md), then link actual [decisions](../decisions/README.md). No participants were studied by this scaffold.
